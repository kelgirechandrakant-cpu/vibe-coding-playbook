# LinkedIn Post Generator — Developer & Founder Marketing Skill

This skill crafts human-sounding, high-converting LinkedIn posts for developer tools, SaaS applications, and AI coding playbooks.

Whenever asked to *"write a linkedin post"*, *"make a linkedin post about X"*, or *"generate a post for linkedin"*, follow this protocol strictly.

---

## 1. Golden Rules of LinkedIn Voice (Strict Anti-Slop)

- **Banned AI Vocabulary:** Never use *delve, game-changer, unlock, leverage, landscape, realm, transformative, spearhead, in today's fast-paced world, testament to, foster, crucial role, exciting news, thrilled to announce*.
- **Line Length:** Keep sentences under 15 words. Keep paragraphs to 1–2 lines maximum. White space drives readability.
- **Tone:** Builder talking to builders, founders, or engineers. Direct, technical, authentic, slightly opinionated.
- **Hook Rules:** The first 2 lines must stop the scroll before the "...see more" fold. Never start with generic greetings (*"Hey connection network!"* or *"I am excited to share..."*).
- **Link Placement Rule:** **Never put URLs in the main post text.** LinkedIn penalizes reach for outbound links in post bodies. Always place the link in the **"💬 Link in first comment"** section.

---

## 2. Structural Hook Formulas

### Formula 1: The False Binary Dissolve
> *"Everyone thinks you have to choose between [Option A] and [Option B].*  
> *After [building X / handling 10k users], we realized both approaches are broken.*  
> *Here is the 3rd way we built [Feature/Project]:"*

### Formula 2: The Scarce-Shots Math
> *"90% of [Target Audience] make the same critical mistake with [Topic].*  
> *It cost us [X hours / 504 timeouts] before we figured out the fix.*  
> *Here is the exact breakdown:"*

### Formula 3: Controlled A/B Anecdote
> *"We let AI write [Feature A] without constraints. It broke in 3 days.*  
> *Then we forced the AI to follow [Architectural Pattern B].*  
> *The result? Zero export bugs across 25+ themes."*

## 3. Post Output Template Format

```markdown
### 📱 LinkedIn Post Draft

[HOOK - First 2 lines]

[BODY - Short 1-2 line paragraphs with whitespace]

[CORE TAKEAWAY / BULLET POINTS]
- Point 1
- Point 2
- Point 3

[CALL TO ACTION - Directing to first comment]

---
### 💬 First Comment (Put the Link Here)
[Link + short comment callout]
```

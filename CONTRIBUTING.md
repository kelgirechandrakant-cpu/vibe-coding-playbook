# Contributing to the Vibe Coding Playbook

We welcome contributions from developers who are actively building software with AI agents. Our goal is to curate a practical, battle-tested playbook—not an artificial collection of generic AI-generated prompts.

## What We Are Looking For

1. **New Skills**: Modular markdown files that give AI agents specific personas (e.g., database modeling, performance auditing).
2. **Better Prompts**: Structured prompts that force AI to plan and verify, rather than just generate code.
3. **Debugging Patterns**: Concrete examples of escaping AI hallucinations.
4. **Case Studies**: Honest breakdowns of how you used AI to build a feature, including the mistakes and lessons learned.

## Submission Requirements

When submitting a Pull Request, you must explain:
1. **The Problem**: What specific failure loop are you trying to fix?
2. **The Context**: What stack or environment did this occur in?
3. **Recommended Workflow**: How should a developer use this?
4. **Example**: Provide a concrete "Bad vs. Good" example.
5. **Why it Works**: Explain the psychology of why this constrains the AI properly.
6. **Limitations**: Be honest about where this prompt/skill fails.

## Quality Standard
- **Actionable:** Can the reader actually do something with this?
- **Specific:** Is it concrete guidance rather than generic AI advice?
- **Honest:** Does it acknowledge AI limitations?

*We prioritize practical experience over AI-generated filler. We will reject PRs that look like ChatGPT wrote them without human testing.*
